### 💻 NSF REU: Smart and Future Computing (TryCycle Project)
**Research Fellow** | *UNC Charlotte (Woodward Hall Lab)* | *Summer 2026 – Present*
* **System Focus:** Co-designing and evaluating **TryCycle**, an LLM-powered educational tool designed to scaffold the "Design Recipe" (problem understanding, data definition, signature/purpose, templates, implementation, testing) in introductory Python courses (CS1/CS2). 
* **HCI & UX Intervention:** Iterating on system prompts and UI presentation to transition the tool from a linear system to a highly iterative "design staircase." Refining feedback mechanisms to be concise, visually annotated, and strategically timed to encourage students to iterate on previous steps before writing code.
* **Experimental Design & Methodology:** Architecting a lab study using a **Pre-Test / Mid-Task (with App) / Post-Test** framework to evaluate the generalization of computational thinking skills without the app. Implementing a *Think-Aloud Protocol*, recording/transcribing sessions, and tracking telemetry metrics (completion time, number of LLM feedback retries).
* **Academic Ownership:** Authoring an institutional IRB amendment detailing the user study parameters and progressive task selection (conditionals, lists, iteration). Conducting an extensive literature review and annotated bibliography spanning CS Education (CSEd), computational problem-solving, and algorithmic design structures.

### 🎨 HCI Research & Co-Design: The Moral Missions Project
**Researcher** | *UW Social Futures Lab* | *Jan. 2025 – Present*
* **The Research Question:** Investigated what types of interactive tools best empower data scientists and AI practitioners to integrate personal and community-based moral values into concrete technical decisions.
* **Methodology & Analysis:** Conducted a thematic analysis of 41 grounded design goals gathered from 15 qualitative interviews with responsible data scientists. Utilized digital affinity diagramming to cluster findings into four core operational themes.
* **Rapid Prototyping:** Facilitated interactive critique sessions by rapidly wireframing and building **30+ custom prototypes** in Figma to solve domain-specific challenges (e.g., communicating model uncertainty).
* **Platform Development:** Collaborated with PhD student Teanna Barrett to design a responsible data science platform mockup to host open-source tool implementations and facilitate future community user testing.
