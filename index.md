# About Me

I am a senior majoring in Computer Science and Linguistics at the University of Washington. My research sits at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW)**, and **Information Science**. I'm interested in studying the sociotechnical dynamics of community-driven knowledge spaces, exploring how groups design and utilize documentation and handle collaborative communication.

Currently, I operate as the **Research Project Manager** for the Svoboda Diaries Project at the University of Washington. This summer, I am pursuing research at the **NSF REU in Smart and Future Computing** at UNC Charlotte.

**I am actively preparing applications for HCI, HCDE, and Information Science PhD programs for the Fall 2027 intake.**
